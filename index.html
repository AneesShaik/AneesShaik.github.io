The Story

Four roles, one thread — building platforms where the secure path is also the fastest one.

01 Senior DevOps Engineer · Gannett Co. · 2017–2020

Learn to automate

Kept a CMS for 100+ publications shipping.

Challenge: A CMS serving 100+ publications and millions of daily readers needed faster, safer releases. What I did: Built CI/CD pipelines and automated environment provisioning, then co-architected a model-based regression framework. What changed: ~1,000 nightly tests caught problems before readers ever saw them.

02 Senior DevOps Engineer · CSSI LLC · 2020–2022

Learn to secure

Cut patch night from 48 hours to under 6.

Challenge: Monthly patch cycles ran nearly two full days, and security scanning happened too late to matter. What I did: Embedded automated scanning into CI/CD and rebuilt the patch pipeline for zero-downtime rollouts. What changed: Patch night dropped to under 6 hours, and production vulnerabilities fell ~80%.

03 Senior Engineering Manager · CSSI LLC · 2022–2024

Learn to move

Moved 350 servers to AWS, from pitch to cutover in 18 months.

Challenge: Leadership needed a case for leaving on-prem — and a team that could actually execute it. What I did: Pitched the migration, then led architecture, security, and compliance design through cutover. What changed: 350 servers moved to AWS with zero unplanned downtime, on an 18-month timeline I set myself.

04 Principal DevSecOps Architect · CSSI LLC · 2024–Present

Learn to scale

Made compliance a pull request.

Challenge: 15+ federal applications, a $4.3M AWS portfolio, and compliance requirements that couldn't keep slowing delivery down. What I did: Turned FISMA, FedRAMP, and NIST 800-53 controls into policy-as-code, led 8 engineers, and sat on the AI Governance Committee. What changed: Cut AWS spend ~19% ($570K → $463K) while compliance checks ran inside the pipeline, not after it.

How I Lead

I own hiring, mentorship, and sprint health for my team, sustaining a 90% sprint success rate. I partner with GRC, SOC, and Audit to keep compliance inside the pipeline instead of after it — work that helped cut manual compliance reporting from hours to 30 minutes each sprint — and helped shape our AI acceptable-use guardrails. Earlier in my career, I also contributed to the technical roadmap behind a winning multi-million-dollar federal modernization proposal.

Earlier: Data Migration Engineer at the International Food Policy Research Institute, and DevOps Engineer at REAN Cloud (AWS Partner), 2016–2017.
